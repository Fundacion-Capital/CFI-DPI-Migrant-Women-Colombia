# Journal requirements and formatting decisions

Checked 14 November 2026. The previously accepted order is retained. Initial format settings are provisional, not a verified submission profile or a new journal decision.

| Order | Official evidence and verified requirements | What changes only at submission preparation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. **Information Technology for Development** (Taylor & Francis) | The [journal page](https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/titd20) confirms scope and editor screening followed by anonymous expert review. The [journal-specific instructions endpoint](https://www.tandfonline.com/action/authorSubmission?journalCode=titd20&page=instructions) did not yield readable instructions in this review. The [publisher layout guide](https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/publishing-your-research/writing-your-paper/journal-manuscript-layout-guide/) supports a provisional 12-point serif, double-spaced draft. The [publisher template guidance](https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/publishing-your-research/writing-your-paper/formatting-and-templates/) says LaTeX and format-free eligibility depend on the journal. | Verify original research article type, exact length and counting rules, abstract/keywords, citations, author anonymity, acceptable upload types, ethics/data/AI statements and OA/charges directly from the accessible journal instructions or portal. Do not infer double-anonymous review from reviewer anonymity, or claim PDF/TeX upload is accepted. If required, prepare Word or a permitted format from the reviewed source; do not adopt an unrelated legacy archive template. |
| 2. **Development Policy Review** (Wiley) | [Author guidelines](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14677679/homepage/forauthors.html): 8,000 words including appendices, footnotes and graphics, excluding abstract and references; structured abstract up to 300 words (Motivation, Purpose, Approach and methods, Findings, Policy implications); current APA; Word or TeX. | Prepare the five-part policy abstract and recount per its own rules; verify submission/anonymity and disclosure details at the candidate checkpoint. |
| 3. **International Migration** (Wiley) | [Author guidelines](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14682435/homepage/forauthors.html): 8,000 words including abstract, policy implications and references; 150-word abstract, up to seven keywords; double-blind; TeX accompanied by a review PDF and supporting files. | Reframe for migration rather than payment-system design alone; verify exhibit limits, current portal and exact anonymous materials before use. |
| 4. **Migration and Development** (Sage) | [Author instructions](https://journals.sagepub.com/author-instructions/mad): original articles 5,000–7,000 words including references, unstructured 150–200-word abstract, four to six keywords, APA references; Word preferred. | Prepare a shorter candidate and approved Word export if this outlet is used, with updated data availability, conflicts, and any other required disclosures. |

The longer academic master and separate technical supplement preserve substance without treating 15,000 words or 50 pages as a target-journal allowance. Keep the title page, funding, acknowledgements, author contributions, interests, ethics/consent, data availability and AI-assistance disclosure separable from the substantive source. A masked review package needs an independent check of self-identification, file/PDF metadata, funding details, appendix content, links and third-party material. Do not hide a material ethics or data issue for anonymization. Neither current APCs, waiver eligibility, turnaround nor permissions to release are verified by this source-formatting step.
