## Important scientific finding
Already emitted category tables imply a singleton-indicator separation problem
in the original full-category MLE nominations. The proof does not require another
participant load or a knowingly doomed fit. The proposed model-only context
groupings deliberately amend adjustment detail. They have NOT been adopted or
run, and may still leave outcome-specific or multivariable separation/rank
problems. A finite result must never be obtained by silently accepting Stata's
perfect-predictor observation removal.

Working robust covariance is not network-adjusted uncertainty. This package
does not claim the unrecorded referral/household dependence has been solved.
Conditional p-values/intervals, if later adopted and computable, are not a
validated population error-control guarantee. Six-slot Holm preserves the
accepted family but cannot repair invalid elementary tests.
