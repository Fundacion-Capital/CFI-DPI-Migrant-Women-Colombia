# D1_rights: historical consent evidence-and-limitations checklist

Prepared: 24 October 2026 (Europe/Berlin). Status: **PREPARED_FOR_REVIEW_NOT_ACCEPTED**.

Companion to the [historical consent account](D1_RIGHTS_HISTORICAL_CONSENT_ACCOUNT_2026-10-24.md). This is a review checklist, not an institutional attestation, accepted response, consent exercise or public-release approval. Every box below is intentionally unmarked. Completing a documentary check does not resolve the associated rights or execution gate.

Evidence labels: **V** = retained document directly inspected; **A** = direct confirmation by the user/one author; **P** = earlier Outlook observation, not an exported or freshly verified email record; **U** = unresolved. Detailed source locators and fingerprints S01-S04 are in the account.

## 1. Author accuracy review

- [ ] **C01 - Attributable scope (A/U):** Confirm that the account correctly attributes confirmations to one author and is limited to the quantitative survey. Do not treat it as the other author's signed agreement or evidence for qualitative/media permissions. Reviewer: each author separately.
- [ ] **C02 - Consent sequence (A):** Check that the explanation and recorded agreement preceded the survey, participation was voluntary and benefits were unaffected. These are author confirmations, not verified participant-level compliance. Reviewer: authors.
- [ ] **C03 - Administration (A/U):** Confirm Spanish, assisted administration, mixed modes, telephone predominance, and the reported SurveyCTO/physical/telephone records. Do not infer exact mode counts or retained audio for every telephone consent. Reviewer: authors; record custodian for later verification.
- [ ] **C04 - Oral public-sharing explanation (A/U):** Confirm that all respondents received the spoken public-download explanation. Preserve that exact historical wording has not been recovered and the explanation was not in the retained written template. Reviewer: authors.
- [ ] **C05 - No inferred separate sharing choice (A/V/U):** Confirm that an explanation to everyone is not being represented as a separately recorded, universally affirmative public-sharing choice. Reconcile S01's optional secondary-use provision with actual practice. Reviewer: authors and institutional governance.
- [ ] **C06 - Requests (A/U):** Confirm the author's report of no withdrawal/deletion/sharing-restriction requests to the time of that report. Do not assert a verified register, permanent absence of requests or loss of withdrawal rights. Reviewer: authors; relevant request custodian.

Do not publish identifiers or infer a violation solely from the draft. Reviewer: institutional data custodian/governance.
- [ ] **C10 - Submission-stage chronology (V/U):** Preserve S02/S03's anticipation of authorization before collection. Do not turn proposed safeguards into implemented facts. Reviewer: institutional research governance.

No participant records were loaded or exported in this task. Reviewer: authorized custodian; institutional governance.

Original management records remain unverified. Reviewer: authorized institutional representative.
- [ ] **C15 - CFI support outside email (A/U):** Use the user's final clarification: support for academic publication arose outside email. Do not describe it as a located written permission or seek an email asserted not to exist. Reviewer: authors; institution/CFI only through separately authorized processes if needed.
- [ ] **C16 - Pre-submission checkpoint (A/V/U):** Preserve the author-confirmed requirement for CFI approval before initial journal submission; distinguish it from S04's review/comment wording. No manuscript-specific approval has been established. Reviewer: authors and responsible institutional/funder representatives.

A licence to Accion is not blanket public release permission. Do not infer an amendment or fully countersigned agreement. Reviewer: responsible institutional authority.
- [ ] **C18 - Joint authors' release approval (A/U):** Require both authors' agreement to the eventual named derivative/package. The reported delegation and decision rule are not package-specific approvals already granted. Reviewer: both authors; institutional confirmation of delegation remains separate.

## 4. Data stewardship and future determination

- [ ] **C19 - Access, custody and retention (A/U):** Document authorized custodians, restricted originals/contact information, consent evidence, retention/deletion rules and withdrawal handling without putting those records into Git. Existing restriction reports do not constitute a current access-control audit. Reviewer: institutional data custodian/governance.
- [ ] **C20 - Disclosure assessment (U):** Before any release, assess a precisely identified derivative for direct and indirect identification, rare combinations, geography, free text, metadata and output disclosure. Identifier removal alone is not a passed anonymization audit. Reviewer: appropriately qualified institutional reviewers; no such test is run here.
- [ ] **C21 - Access and reuse terms (U):** Determine whether open access is appropriate or safeguarded/controlled access is necessary; distinguish any intended academic reuse from the practical unrestricted audience of GitHub. No open licence is selected. Reviewer: institutional governance and both authors.
- [ ] **C22 - Need for additional consent (U):** Obtain an institutional assessment of whether the historical basis supports the exact proposed use and whether further consent or another lawful/ethical route is needed. Do not presume either answer or manufacture retrospective approval. Reviewer: authorized ethics/data-governance body.
- [ ] **C23 - New wording boundary (U):** If a new information sheet/addendum is prepared, date/version it honestly, mark it prospective and unadministered, retain S01 and distinguish drafting from actual consent. No participant recontact is authorized by this checklist. Reviewer: institutional governance and authors.
- [ ] **C24 - Manuscript statements (U):** Match ethics, consent, data availability, funding and publication-rights statements to established evidence and actual approval dates/scopes. Never claim historical committee approval or that a reconstructed script was used verbatim. Reviewer: both authors; institutional governance and CFI as applicable.
