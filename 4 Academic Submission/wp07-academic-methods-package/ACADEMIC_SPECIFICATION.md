# Consolidated academic quantitative specification

5 November 2026. Source-only consolidation; no participant or analytical execution.
This is the current handoff for the bounded WP07 package, not a replacement of
dated evidence or a claim that WP06/WP07 or the parent scientific gates passed.

## 1. Research questions and inferential scope

The quantitative strand describes how the recruited women receive, retain and
use remittances and domestic digital services; how selected access, reported
capability and administrative conditions are associated with engagement; and
how transaction experiences, recourse, safety, agency, social attitudes and
enabling conditions qualify that engagement. Conditional profiling asks whether
coherent response patterns add information beyond these direct descriptions.
None of these questions estimates a causal effect of DPI or program participation.

The unit is the woman respondent; some eligibility and remittance questions
refer to the household. The accepted frame includes international migrants and
Colombian return migrants in recruited urban/peri-urban communities. Prior
program participation, personal phone ownership and literacy are not eligibility
requirements. There is no national sampling probability, verified referral
network or adopted RDS/survey weight. Preserve the historical reference cohort
of 423 and all manual test/non-official and coded exclusions. No reinstatement,
new filter, household deduplication, personal-recipient restriction or foreign-
nationality/five-year restriction is introduced by this package.

## 3. Versions, geography, time and missingness

Five recorded versions are retained: 2512090157, 2512091719, 2512111044,
2512121811 and 2602120658. Use actual version-specific meanings and offered
options. The first two forms' residence code 4 is Other; later code 4 is Soacha;
Meta is an additional later option. The prepared representation keeps Bogotá,
Cali, Medellín, early Other, later Soacha and Meta distinct. No city pooling,
uniform old code-4 meaning or municipality within Meta is inferred.

Historical recorded interview dates span 9 December 2025-17 February 2026 in
prior aggregate checks. This is not an administrative closure date. Preserve
raw/coded clocks and the unresolved discrepancy; do not normalize timezones or
use today's year. First arrival to live in Colombia is not latest return or
continuous residence. The IVS time source and fixed construction bounds remain
unresolved. The extension's calendar-history representation is not fixed here.

Six source states remain distinct: ordinary valid, structural skip, unavailable
in version, applicable nonresponse, item-specific sentinel and unresolved.
Codes 98/99 have no global numerical meaning. A sentinel may be a meaningful
category, not a zero, invalid record or cohort exclusion. Malformed/contradictory
multiselect answers require review; export dummy zeros do not establish that an
unavailable option was considered and rejected.

Item reports use their declared version/route-specific bases, showing the
admitted, applicable, recorded, ordinary and sentinel coverage relevant to the
question. Multiselect shares may sum above 100%. An unoffered option has no
denominator or invented zero. Empty bases produce undefined percentages. A
candidate uses every declared scorable component; its own nonmissing N is shown
alongside component coverage and common-observation comparisons. No global
complete-case cohort, zero-fill, available-item average or imputation is adopted.

Stage types are not interchangeable. The exact raw TARGET33/preparation and
item-reporting inputs contain both numeric fields and string multiselect
parents. The old preparation draft's numeric-only input comment is inaccurate:
its q4_12 expression and P1's explicit string check establish the mixed type.
Only the new fourteen-field prepared readiness view is wholly numeric. No
destringing or alternative projection is authorized to repair that old comment.

## 5. Four conditional academic model nominations

Primary outcomes are wp07_remit_any_digital (q6_13 codes 2-5 versus all-cash
withdrawal code 1, with the five original categories retained) and
wp07_domestic_60d (q6_14 yes/no; refusal/nonresponse unscored). The remittance
question's personal wording remains a limitation inside a household-eligible
frame. The latest receipt channel q6_4 is a different concept, not a substitute.

R0 and D0 nominate IAT3, IADT2 and IAFF2, continuous age, categorical education
and version-aware residence. R1 and D1 nominate the corresponding extension
with nominal occupation and first-arrival calendar history after its provenance,
representation and coverage are resolved. These are four model targets, not
four authorized fits or a license for a supporting regression grid.

The nominated reference labels map to education code 5 (completed secondary),
prepared residence code 1 (Bogotá), and occupation code 3 (employee/worker).
The codebook verifies those labels; model-specific support remains unknown.
The occupation label does not certify formal/stable employment. No default
pooling or dropping is a remedy for sparse support or separation.

Age/form domains and nonmissing screens do not certify a model sample. MODEL_READINESS
records needed outcome variation, category/covariate support, precision, overlap
and sample continuity. The new readiness script reports only restricted aggregate
screens; it does not make an estimator's omissions, select a final sample or fit.

## 6. Inference and interpretation

Binary logit is the conditional estimator nomination. Probability summaries
average over the relevant model sample's observed covariates, not an artificial
mean respondent or a national population. Exact supported contrasts must be
fixed before fitting. IAFF has possible values 0, .5 and 1; an arbitrary .1
change is not an observed administrative transition. Coefficients/native evidence
remain in the restricted technical record if estimation is later authorized.

The accepted primary family contains six overall conditional association tests:
three focal constructs across the two main models. It is one family, with Holm
step-down adjustment at nominal 5%, conditional on defensible underlying tests.
Pointwise 95% intervals are distinct from family-wise testing. The handling of
nonestimable tests must be fixed without silently shrinking the family.

Leader/referral/possible household dependence is not solved merely by specifying
robust standard errors. Neither an enumerator/city cluster nor invented network
or household link is adopted. The final variance method, exact probability
contrasts and unavailable-test implementation remain open. No executable logit,
margins, bootstrap or p-value block is written with an implicit default.

The preserved 19-entry comparison register is carried forward, entry by entry,
in COMPARISON_HANDOFF.csv. Its measurement/precision/coverage/specification/
uncertainty/provenance comparisons are not 19 significance tests. Older pending
proposal flags do not negate the later accepted conditional model nominations.
PRIMARY_TEST_FAMILY.csv supplies the separate six-slot current primary family;
all p-value fields are NOT_COMPUTED, not zero or manufactured missing results.

## 7. Profiling, integration and paper-facing reporting

The academic LCA is optional and conditional. Historical candidate/final states,
failures and discrepancies are preserved. Accepted IURD/IEDF item accounts do
not supply scalar replacements for historical H1. Family, population, missingness,
count/start/optimization plan, identification, local-dependence, interpretation
and classification-error treatment must be specified before a new fit manifest.
No modal assignment is error-free, and shared/class-defining items cannot serve
as independent external validation. No quantitative result will rewrite the
coauthor's qualitative evidence to fit a desired typology.

The methods draft and EXHIBIT_PLAN distinguish existing historical statements,
planned academic outputs and currently unavailable estimates. All fifteen domains
remain traceable across article/supplement destinations. No figure, result or
coefficient is generated or inserted, and no existing main.tex or bibliography
is edited. The retained journal sequence is unchanged; journal-specific final
requirements belong to a later refreshed submission check.
