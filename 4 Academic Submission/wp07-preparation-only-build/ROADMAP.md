This is the working sequence from the audited WP07 contract through M13
and a separate conference-ready branch. It supplements, not replaces,

## Starting point and the critical path

The authoritative academic foundation is the full legacy draft labelled
31 July 2026, not the shortened CFI or Canva versions. Keep the latter,
plus the policy brief and technical and benchmark companions, as evidence
of prior dissemination and presentation choices. The existing
`main.tex` is a preparation record, not a finished article. Do not
overwrite that record or sync to Overleaf during this contract stage.

The paper's organising question is how **access to digital finance relates
to usable remittance value and domestic digital participation** for the
women studied. These are distinct outcomes. Separate account access,
receipt channels, what recipients do with funds, and domestic use.
Describe associations for the observed sample. Do not call this a causal
estimate of DPI, an estimate for all migrant women, or a comparison of
national populations. Treat mechanisms raised in interviews and the
institutional comparison as distinct evidence, not proof of mediation.

### 2. Close quantitative results and exhibits — E.3–E.5 / M2, M6, M8

1. **Describe sample and outcomes first.** Read only newly approved
   aggregate sources. Build T1 cohort/version accounting and T2/F1 observed
   remittance disposition and 60-day domestic participation. Use the
   [descriptive build sheet](../wp07-e5-v3-logging-native-validation/DESCRIPTIVE_BUILD.md)
   to populate S1–S9. Reconcile route/applicable, recorded, ordinary,
   sentinel, unresolved and relevant model Ns. Preserve early/later form
   meanings and the distinction between unoffered and observed zero.
   Add a minimal scoped no-fit extraction only for demonstrable missing
   cells; never treat a suppressed or undefined value as zero.
2. **Decide what associations add.** If AS1 is retained, qualify an exact
   three-field, no-fit selection for IAT3/IADT2/IADT3 and distinguish pair-
   from common-complete comparisons. Show coefficients only when N and
   variance support them. This is not the historical 15-score C2 matrix,
   and cannot remove its hold. If the evidence adds little, retain an
   explicit supplement/retirement decision rather than force a result.
3. **Use the five existing E.4 working models where justified.** R0/D0 are
   the two primary contexts; R1/D1 are calendar/occupation extensions and
   R0C the common-base comparison. Reconcile mask and estimator Ns,
   group/reference support, convergence/omissions, six primary tests with
   fixed-six Holm, fixed probability contrasts and pointwise intervals.
   Keep the independent-record working covariance limitation. Establish
   lineage to the in-memory E.4 predecessor; new saved candidate validation
   alone does not rebase coefficients. No refit for a more favourable result.
   Any necessary change needs a reason, exact scope and new fit authority.
4. **Resolve the independent E.5 hold.** Get an approved destination and
   provider route, then an exact grant for the five sealed aggregate
   inputs and one no-fit render. Audit QT1/QF1/QT2/QS1/QS2/QS3 against the
   exact ledgers, verify tables/intervals/axes/rounding and check all pages.
   Hold cells that need disclosure review. If the route stays blocked,
   continue source drafting and mark unverified results, not substitute
   legacy numbers or synthetic output.
5. **Disposition before writing from numbers.** For each legacy object
   and proposed replacement, record retain, rebuild, move to supplement,

   format and disclosure status. Adopt claims only against approved evidence.

### 3. Complete the evidence and writing lanes — B–D / G.1–G.7

These lanes can move now without records, new analysis, access to restricted
ledgers or an institutional assumption. Plan three to four working weeks
of drafting in parallel with the quantitative work, then one to two for
integrated revision. Do not make quantitative findings a prerequisite for
writing the question, literature, design, or documentary institutional context.

- **B: literature and Colombia chronology (M3).** Start from the
  [WP05 source ledger](../wp05-literature-context/README.md), the verified
  public NotebookLM catalogue and original Zotero/issuer publications.
  Resolve missing full texts and citation metadata for claims we keep;
  distinguish nearest evidence from broad background. Trace documentation
  and onboarding, connectivity, intermediation, protection and autonomy
  to the argument. Date regulation, implementation, actual reach and
  survey periods separately. Explain how national indicators and survey
  denominators differ. Deliver a claim/citation ledger and the revised
  introduction, theory and context; no claim that interviewees used a
  system because it was announced or available.
- **C: migrant qualitative and expert evidence (M10).** Recover the authored
  findings, caveats, variation and placement of quotations from the full
  draft. Use the [joint evidence display](../wp07-e5-v3-logging-native-validation/MIXED_EVIDENCE.md)
  to pair evidence only on comparable propositions; label complementarity,
  divergence and not measured. Describe participant and expert units
  separately. Obtain Angélica's review of paraphrases, translation, quote
  selection, confidentiality and interpretation. Do not recode transcripts,
  add voices, infer frequency, or map respondents to classes.
- **D: institutions, policy and regional benchmark (M10).** Verify each
  retained country-by-dimension comparison against dated primary sources.
  Separate law/design, rollout, actual reach, complaint routes, settlement,
  cash-out and cross-border functionality. Keep unknown and incomparable
  cells explicit. Use comparable features to motivate implementation
  choices, not a league table or evidence of causal policy transfer.
  Route detailed benchmark provenance to the supplement and make
  recommendations conditional on the evidence and responsible actor.
- **F: decide LCA's role (M9).** Recommendation: keep LCA off the main
  paper's critical path and default to the observed domains unless authors
  find a clear incremental contribution. Resolve ML01 first; if no LCA,
  record why and retire class labels and class-specific inference. If
  retained, resolve ML02–ML05 on population/inputs, bounded enumeration,
  identification/classification uncertainty and downstream inference
  before any new fit. A historical class count or good fit criterion
  alone is not a validation. Document any schedule/scope consequence.
- **G: assemble one article (M1–M2/M11).** Freeze the approved legacy
  source and evidence map; keep the technical supplement distinct. Draft
  in order: research question and contribution; theory and chronology;
  design, recruitment, measurement, estimands and limitations; observed
  outcomes and model findings; migrant and expert evidence; regional
  context; discussion, implications, limitations and conclusion. Draft a
  short claim-based abstract after the results lock. Every exhibit and
  quote needs provenance and a disposition. Explain retrospective
  revisions rather than calling them preregistered. Make methods and
  provenance reviewable without circulating the private dataset.

### 4. Referee review, reproducibility, and clearance — H.1–H.5 / M0, M12–M13

1. **Independent evidence pass:** verify dataset and output lineage,
   exact-version code, reproducible artifacts and supporting bibliography.
   Scope any restricted rebuild separately; reproduce the outputs that are
   actually retained. Audit number/denominator/units, confidence intervals,
   labels, stale text, quote consent, geography and contextual dates.
2. **Referee pass:** challenge novelty, sample generalizability, version
   changes, missingness, support/dependence, robustness, triangulation and
   policy extrapolation. Maintain a disposition log: fix at source, qualify,
   move, omit, or open a specific evidence request. Recheck edited claims;
   avoid cosmetic changes to statistical cells.
3. **Reader-facing artifact pass:** produce and visually check actual
   article and supplement PDFs, references, captions, cross-references,
   accessibility, table breaks and anonymity of review copies. Check
   approved repository contents, Git history, figure/TeX/PDF metadata
   and versioned links separately. No unscreened DTA, small cells,
   identifying quotations or access promises in a deposit.
4. **Rights and decisions:** resolve the exact ethics/consent/reuse/data-
   sharing statements and institutional route from D1_rights/R01–R12.
   Do not describe an oral review as external approval, certify unsupported
   consent coverage, or assume the narrowed package was already approved.
   Resolve the historic exposure/remediation decision and actual disclosure
   review. Agree on restricted retention, who may see which artifacts, and
   what to do if a venue asks for records that cannot be released.
5. **Versioned approval:** both authors approve the exact pre-CFI draft.
   Obtain CFI approval before initial journal submission, then have both
   authors approve the revised final text; use the agreed first/corresponding
   author route. Separately approve the exact replication package or record
   why it is withheld. Any abstracts, decks, recordings or circulated
   drafts get the external-use checks relevant to their own exact version.
   Preserve approval records, and do not contact anyone on this plan alone.

Allow two to three working weeks for internal review/revision after the
integrated draft; institutional, coauthor and CFI turnaround is a dependency,
not a promised date. A final version cannot advance by calendar alone.

*Migration and Development*. Adapt one scientific core to the first venue;
do not prepare or send four submissions in parallel. For ITD, foreground

implementation and institutional design; for migration venues, foreground
migrant livelihoods, status, remittances and situated agency. These are
editorial adaptations, not alternative analyses.
