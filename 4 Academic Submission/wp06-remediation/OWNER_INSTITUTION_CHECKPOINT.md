# WP06 researcher and institutional response packet

This is a preparation packet, not an approval request already answered, ethics record or execution contract. All24 entries in response_template.json remain blank and unapproved. Copy any real response into a new dated, restricted successor record; do not amend sealed historical packets. Cite restricted evidence by reference ID/path and date, not by pasting participant records or confidential institutional documents into a public file. No email is sent and no person is contacted by this packet.

For every response, specify the decision ID, responsible person/role, date, choice, evidence reference, rationale, limitations and affected outputs. `evidence_review_complete` means only that the respondent has documented a review; it is not an automatic approval, ethics certification or permission to fit. A request for evidence or deferral is a valid explicit outcome. Inconsistent or unresolved decisions keep the relevant gate closed. An institutional/author signature cannot substitute for actual participant compliance evidence; a statistical receipt cannot substitute for institutional authority.

For each construct choose `retain_historical`, `revise_recipe`, `exclude_from_primary` or `defer`, with a substantive rationale. Retention is not proof of validity. Revision requires a versioned recipe reference, routing/missingness/denominator policy, validation-plan reference and affected-output map **before** implementation. Rejecting or deferring a construct does not authorize silent deletion from the historical evidence. Preserve source-exact historical outputs and disclose academic changes as retrospective.

## What is accepted now

Only hypothetical ART-labelled responses are used to test metadata structure. The validator rejects real-origin responses, blanket/unknown choices, wrong roles, missing evidence/date/rationale and unversioned revision proposals. A structurally valid hypothetical response still returns `STRUCTURE_ONLY_NOT_REAL_APPROVAL` and cannot activate anything. Real decisions must be reviewed in a separate successor; the artificial checker is not an institutional authorization system.

The next decision checkpoint needs the authors' population/measurement/reporting responses and the institution's rights/ethics evidence, not a blanket “approve everything.” No participant or estimation activity is unlocked by copying or filling this template.
