## R03 — Consolidated consent evidence and coverage account

**For your review only.** This account separates your confirmations, findings from the retained documents reviewed earlier, and unresolved limitations. It incorporates your corrections and does not change any dossier, packet or institutional decision.

### 1. Quantitative consent process — your confirmations

For all quantitative surveys, whether conducted by telephone or in person, enumerators explained the study, answered questions and obtained the woman’s affirmative agreement before beginning the substantive questions. They recorded that agreement in the **SurveyCTO consent field**, with no known exceptions.

Your corrected account is that:

- **Quantitative consent was documented only in SurveyCTO.** There were no quantitative paper consent forms and no separate audio recordings of quantitative consent.
- **Signed paper participation/consent documents belong to the qualitative interviews and focus groups.** You confirm those documents are preserved. Their contents, completeness and coverage have not been inspected in this R03 process.

This correction supersedes the earlier descriptions suggesting that in-person quantitative respondents signed physical consent forms. It also clarifies that “recorded telephone consent” meant recording the response in SurveyCTO—not making an audio recording.

### 2. Public-data-sharing explanation — your confirmations

You confirm that, from the beginning of actual quantitative fieldwork, all enumerators were instructed to explain the proposed public sharing before recording consent. The same explanation was given for telephone and in-person interviews.

According to your account, women were told that:

- An anonymized version of their **individual survey responses** could be made publicly downloadable for other researchers.
- Access would be **publicly available online**, not restricted to researchers who had received approval.
- Names, telephone numbers and other identifying details would be excluded.

There was **one agreement covering participation and the explained data uses**, rather than a separate public-sharing choice. The SurveyCTO consent field should therefore be described as recording the participation agreement following the explanation—not as a distinct, separately recorded public-sharing authorization.

Enumerators explained these matters **in their own words following training**. No fixed script is retained. They answered questions and received affirmative agreement, but there was **no formal comprehension check**. This supports describing the process you report; it does not support claiming that identical wording was used or that understanding was formally tested.

You do not know whether women were specifically told that copies already downloaded by others might not be recoverable or removable. **That point remains unresolved.**

### 3. Training and fieldwork supervision — your confirmations

You recall an approximately **three-hour recorded training session**, during which enumerators went through the procedures and provided feedback.

You also confirm that coordinators verified delivery of the public-sharing explanation through **direct confirmation with enumerators during fieldwork**. That confirmation was oral, and you do not have a retained written record of it.

The verification method must consequently be described precisely: it was **enumerator confirmation to coordinators**, not direct observation of every interview, respondent confirmation, or a retained interview-level compliance checklist.
