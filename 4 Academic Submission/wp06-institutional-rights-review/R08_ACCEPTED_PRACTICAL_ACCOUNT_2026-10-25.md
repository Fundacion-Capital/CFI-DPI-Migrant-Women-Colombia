## R08 — Practical scope for reviewing the public materials

**For your review only.** The purpose is to check that the materials prepared for publication do not reveal participant information—not to create another elaborate approval process. **You will decide any changes to methodology, results or interpretation.**

### Your choices

The proposed public package will be limited to **the final paper, its selected appendices, and the code and documentation needed to explain and reproduce that work**, subject to the restricted-input limitations already established in R04.

Within that scope:

- **Respondent-level data and auxiliary outputs stay restricted.** This includes individual class assignments, posterior probabilities, fitted predictions and respondent-level data underlying figures.
- **Figures showing individual respondents are excluded unless specifically disclosure-reviewed.** Removing names or point labels alone will not be treated as sufficient.
- **Tables containing very small subgroups or rare combinations are held for specific review.** No arbitrary cell-size threshold has been selected, and we have not concluded that every small cell is unsafe.
- **Embedded identifiers are excluded from public code and documentation.** Exact originals and manual-exclusion rules remain preserved under restricted custody. We will not simply delete exclusion commands or silently change the analytical sample.
- **Historical and exploratory outputs are outside the proposed package** unless separately selected and reviewed. This choice does not itself remove anything already stored or published.
- **The proposed package is considered together, in its reviewed version.** Reviewing one table or figure does not automatically establish that it can safely be combined with every other output or later addition.

These are your scope choices. **They are not findings that particular files have passed review.**

1. Explain the concern and identify the affected material.
2. Distinguish a presentation-only adjustment from a change to the analysis or interpretation.
3. Leave the methodological or results decision to you, preserving the original analysis and output.

**The earlier proposed blanket requirement for joint author review of every such change is not part of this practical R08 account.** This does not assume any separate coauthor or institutional approval, nor alter obligations already established elsewhere.
