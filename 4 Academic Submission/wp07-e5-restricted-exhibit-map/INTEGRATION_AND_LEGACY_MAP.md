## Claims to retain, qualify or retire

Retain the documented adjusted associations and their uncertainty on the actual
model-specific bases. Qualify candidate constructs, item applicability, selection,
cross-sectional timing and independent-record working covariance in the text,
not only footnotes. An interval including zero is not proof of no relationship.

Retire unsupported claims of national prevalence, formal RDS inference, household
independence, causal DPI/Bre-B effects, training effects, validated empowerment,
verified fraud/protection, transitions through a financial-inclusion funnel, or
mechanisms established by one cross-sectional logit. No observed difference in
two p-values proves a difference between two associations. No policy recommendation
may claim an estimated payoff from changing the candidate measures.
