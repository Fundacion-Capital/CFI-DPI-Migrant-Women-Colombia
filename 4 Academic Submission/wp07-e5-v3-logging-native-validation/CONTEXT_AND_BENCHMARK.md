## Colombia and contribution

Separate three stages: a household's incoming cross-border transfer, the recipient's access to and handling of the proceeds, and subsequent domestic digital use. Domestic interoperability can change what can be done with a balance already held digitally; it does not identify the transfer's upstream settlement path or ensure that a particular woman can independently use the balance. The [Banco de la República technical retrospective, phase 5](https://www.banrep.gov.co/sites/default/files/publicaciones/archivos/documento-tecnico-bre-b-febrero-2026.pdf) confirms 6 October 2025 full operation following a pilot, and the [operator FAQ](https://www.banrep.gov.co/es/bre-b/preguntas-frecuentes) specifies domestic payments and transfers. The [accepted fieldwork account](../wp06-author-dossier/D1_POPULATION_ACCEPTED_ACCOUNT_2026-10-19.md) records interviews in the early operation period; calendar proximity is not assigned exposure, a pre/post contrast or evidence of the rail used. PEP and PPT are distinct institutional episodes; retain the dated legal sources already recorded in WP05 and do not infer respondent eligibility or verification.
