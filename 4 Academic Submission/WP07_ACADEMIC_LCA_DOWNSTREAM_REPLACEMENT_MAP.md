Use the historical LCA workflow as an auditable reference, not as the default
academic execution chain. Preserve all original results, failures, source code,
packets and the disabled historical revision. **Retirement means exclusion from
a proposed new academic path, never deletion or rewriting of history.**

retention/use and q6_14 reported domestic digital participation while preserving

**New recommendations, not assumed owner decisions**

- Do not inherit the complete historical grid, automatic benchmarks, large
  overlay testing battery or old report_candidate screen as academic defaults.
- Prefer progress on primary/supporting item-level evidence while the LCA
  contribution is adjudicated.
- If classification is needed, propose permutation-consistent tie handling
  and a second order statistic that includes ties, rather than the old
  second-distinct-value rule. Neither has been applied or adopted.
- Keep reporting selective and complete for the specifications actually
  authorized; do not choose results, model definitions or class counts for
  significance, entropy or a visually attractive typology.

**Still unavailable or unverified**

## 6. Interpretation and software boundaries

The earlier source review demonstrates possible tie-priority, second-distinct
gap and missing-diagnostic screen problems on artificial cases. It does not
show that an actual archived respondent or candidate was affected. Historical
assignments/flags remain unchanged.

Posterior-weighted descriptions are not automatically corrected downstream
inference. StataCorp distinguishes latent-class probabilities from posterior
probabilities conditional on observed responses in its
[prediction manual](https://www.stata.com/manuals/sempredictaftergsem.pdf);
the [lclass options](https://www.stata.com/manuals/semgsemlclassoptions.pdf)
specify model classes and invariance choices. Those capabilities do not
demonstrate compatibility of any proposed BCH/three-step/one-step method with
the selected mixed-input recipe. No method is selected or declared impossible
here; feasibility remains an exact future check.

The original worker is not qualified by the separate successful standalone
StataCorp test. Appendix C2 remains on hold. Nothing was sent to StataCorp,
and no mailbox or native-runtime probe was performed during this map task.
