## 3. Finite validation-wave proposal — not approved

To make the next decision concrete, propose **one family × four class counts (2, 3, 4, 5) × three independent, fixed seed schedules = at most 12 candidate fits**, with **zero retries and zero bootstrap/resampling fits** in that wave. This is a resource-bounded planning judgment, not a literature-derived adequate number of starts, a power calculation, a guarantee of identifying the global maximum, or approval of the historical H1 measurement. The historical final's 600 starts and iteration settings remain historical facts; D5 must explicitly justify/pin academic settings and runtime feasibility. Those settings and seeds are deliberately not guessed or executable now.

This wave would evaluate enumeration/optimization and saved-state behavior, not authorize participant posterior production, empirical residual tests, downstream regressions or broad inferential validation. Those operations require separately named scope even when no fit is involved. Bootstrap likelihood tests or uncertainty resampling require a separate finite budget and an appropriate reference design; zero resampling now does not mean resampling is scientifically unnecessary. There is no reserve/retry budget hidden inside the 12-fit proposal. Any authorized failure or interrupted fit consumes its named attempt; inspect durable status before any dispatch, and never infer a timeout means a new fit is safe.

Before author approval, the exact manifest must list twelve unique recipe/seed IDs, starts/iterations/time/capacity per recipe, expected observations and indicator levels, source/native-capture versions, collision-free restricted outputs, failure handling and shutdown checks. ## 4. Scientific decision consequences

On completion of a later authorized wave, report the entire outcome ledger before selecting a typology. Evaluate optimization consistency, boundary/sparse-category behavior, parameter/probability uncertainty, local dependence and observed-data fit where separately authorized and methodologically appropriate. Information criteria require comparable observed likelihoods/samples/outcome spaces; entropy does not establish the correct class count. A conventional chi-square likelihood-ratio reference is not assumed valid for mixture enumeration. Current Stata documentation describes model and postestimation capabilities, but does not certify version-16 behavior or this model's successful diagnostics: [gsem reference](https://www.stata.com/manuals/semgsem.pdf), [postestimation reference](https://www.stata.com/manuals/semgsempostestimation.pdf), accessed 17 October 2026.

Possible author dispositions are retain with qualified claims, revise under a new bounded contract, retain descriptive-only profiles, reject academic LCA, or remain inconclusive. A negative decision still advances the paper by avoiding unsupported typology claims. No boundary warning mandates automatic class merging, category pooling, regularization or item deletion; each remedy changes assumptions and needs its own rationale and validation. No favorable outcome regression can rescue an unstable measurement model.

For downstream analysis, construction checks and shared-item differences may illustrate what the model encodes; they cannot validate it independently. Truly external comparisons still face confounding, routing, measurement and classification-error issues. Choose and validate an appropriate uncertainty approach before inferential use; hard assignment plus robust regression SEs is not automatically sufficient. An estimated segment outcome in multinomial regression is generated, not directly observed ground truth. Reference categories, samples, nonconvergence, omission/perfect-prediction behavior and multiplicity must remain explicit.
