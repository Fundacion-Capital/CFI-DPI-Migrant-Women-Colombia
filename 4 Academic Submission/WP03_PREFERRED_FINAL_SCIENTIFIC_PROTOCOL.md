## 4. Four combined subprocesses for scientific remediation

### A. Resolve the decision inputs without re-estimation

First consolidate WP04's sample, consent, missingness and measurement issues rather than treating an LCA numerical question in isolation. Bind every indicator to questionnaire/form version, raw item, transformation, denominator and construct. Identify whether tertile thresholds use the full input or an estimation subset, whether ties/sparse categories exist, and whether structural skips were treated as missing, zero or not applicable. Do not recode based on which version improves separation or a downstream coefficient. Any unresolved source discrepancy is a blocker for the affected scientific claim, not an invitation to infer a convenient value.

Create a variable-role table for every downstream measure: class-defining indicator, constituent/shared-item index, external covariate, external outcome or contextual overlay. Reusing an index that supplied a class indicator can demonstrate a profile's construction, but cannot independently validate it. Covariates are not automatically exogenous; cross-sectional associations are not causal effects. Preserve the existing qualitative record and ask the coauthor to adjudicate tensions rather than modifying quotations, themes or qualitative conclusions. This protocol does not claim the complete legacy manuscript, Zotero corpus or institutional context has been newly reviewed.

### B. Lock the bounded scientific validation design before new estimates

WP06 must lock the scientific question, eligible sample, indicator rationale, missing-data treatment and comparison families before WP08 execution. Include all already-seen historical candidate results and failures as retrospective evidence. Do not require four classes because the report used four, and do not select a solution because it produces favorable segment-outcome significance. A simpler solution or no retained LCA is an admissible outcome. Do not conflate k-means/Ward benchmarks built on different inputs with replications of the latent-class model.

The future design must specify, rather than leave to the executor: named candidate families/class counts, finite number of independent seed schedules and starts per candidate, iteration and wall-clock limits, solution-comparison coordinates/tolerances, matching rule for label permutations, permitted resampling design and its total fit ceiling, and precision/power limitations at the available sample size. Those numeric budgets are **not approved in this package**. The historical final's 600 starts, seed and version are historical recipe facts, not sufficient justification for a new scientific search. After a budget expires, stop and retain an inconclusive result; no retries, escalating starts or alternative specifications occur automatically.

Compare repeated solutions using likelihood, probability profiles and diagnostics, not label numbers alone. Match labels only in scratch comparison coordinates, with an explicit predeclared criterion; do not rewrite saved states or participant labels. Agreement across a finite set of starts is evidence of optimization stability, not proof of the global optimum. Predeclare how multiple near-optimal solutions and substantively divergent profiles will be reported. A boundary estimate may be a sparse-data feature, a limiting solution or model strain; the protocol does not diagnose which without the required evidence.

Assess class enumeration jointly through statistical evidence, admissibility, stability, substantive coherence and limitations. Use information criteria only on appropriately comparable likelihoods/samples, keeping item sets and missingness rules visible. Do not rank different outcome spaces by raw AIC/BIC as if identical models. Any likelihood-ratio or bootstrap test must have a justified mixture-specific reference/calibration and a separate bounded fit budget; an ordinary chi-square reference is not silently assumed. Entropy measures classification certainty, not the truth of a class count. No universal entropy or smallest-class cutoff becomes an automatic acceptance rule.

### C. Predefine uncertainty and remedy pathways

If new protected observed-data work is authorized, evaluate posterior uncertainty, class-specific effective support, modal sizes versus mixing weights, influential/sparse response patterns, residual/local-dependence evidence and uncertainty in scientifically interpretable probabilities/contrasts. Determine whether the tools and estimator support the requested diagnostics; a command being documented in current Stata does not prove compatibility under the historical version-16 recipe. All Stata execution must use the Stata MCP interface and a named isolated worker. No participant file is needed to implement the present protocol.

Do not “repair” the huge standard error by truncating the table, clipping a cutpoint, zeroing its variance, merging a class or changing an item until the output looks regular. Candidate academic remedies may include justified recoding of genuinely unsupported categories, a theoretically supported simpler class structure, a revised measurement model, or a carefully justified alternative estimator/regularization. Each changes assumptions and may change the estimand; priors/penalties or category pooling require explicit substantive and sensitivity decisions, not a numerical default. All belong to a separate versioned academic branch of analysis, with old results intact.

For external covariates/distal outcomes, distinguish a one-step joint model that may change class formation from a classification-error-adjusted multistep approach that seeks to preserve the measurement definition. A three-step or BCH-style method is a candidate to assess, not an installed or validated Stata implementation. Its suitability depends on outcome type, covariate role, direct effects/local dependence, sparse cells and error-model assumptions. Simple posterior weighting or repeated pseudo-class draws are not universally valid substitutes for an uncertainty-aware estimator. Conventional robust standard errors on a hard assigned class do not generally account for estimated-class uncertainty. Historical hard-class regressions may be reconstructed as historical evidence, but must not be relabelled uncertainty-corrected academic inference.

Define the exact associative estimand for each retained downstream family, reference categories, eligible sample and missingness treatment. Separate construction checks from external comparisons. Predefine which contrasts form a testing family and how effect sizes, uncertainty, multiplicity and null findings will be shown; do not add controls or change reference groups to secure significance. If only exploratory inference is defensible, label it explicitly and temper recommendations. No downstream covariate, outcome or policy narrative can rescue an unstable measurement solution by interpretation alone.

### D. Adjudicate and report, including a negative decision

At the end of the authorized future evidence package, record one of: retain with qualified probability/profile claims; revise and revalidate under an approved academic contract; retain descriptive-only typology while withholding inferential overlays; or do not retain an academic LCA. An unresolved/inconclusive disposition is also valid and keeps the gate held. Each decision requires links to the full candidate/failure ledger, mathematical and observed-data evidence, sample/measurement rationale, sensitivity results and limitations, with named author sign-off. No individual diagnostic is sufficient, and numeric acceptance thresholds cannot be invented after seeing the new results.

The final decision table must distinguish completed historical replay, scientific recommendation, owner/author approval, participant-processing permission and release approval. A technical verifier can establish file integrity and exact schema coverage, not construct validity. Claims of global identification, regular cutpoint inference, robust participant assignment or causal segment effects remain withheld unless supported by their own evidence; most are not established by the present design. Record changes from this dated protocol with reasons and timing; never overwrite the lock to make a deviation appear prespecified.
