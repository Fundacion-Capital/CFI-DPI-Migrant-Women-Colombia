## 3. Proposed D4: a finite, substantive analysis

### Questions and evidence tiers

The recommended core is a descriptive/associative account of how surveyed women engage digitally with remittances and domestic transactions, which reported conditions/capabilities are associated with those forms of engagement, and what conditional experiences/constraints accompany them. The observational unit is a woman, not a certified distinct household. Snowball/contact/referral recruitment does not support national design-based inference, formal RDS weights or causal DPI/training effects.

Propose two central outcomes:

- **P1:** `q6_13` digital retention/use rather than all cash withdrawal, retaining the original five categories. The accepted binary does not measure a share, latest receipt channel or personal receipt alone.
- **P2:** `q6_14` 60-day domestic digital participation, keeping refusal/nonresponse unscored. Its denominator is not restricted to the affirmative branch used for transaction experiences.

Supporting evidence consists of selected transaction-experience items; digital problems and conditional redress; and separate safety/agency reports. The role register names the source fields. It does not propose fitting every follow-up or every survey item. Other receipt/use context, trust/preferences, learning/support, barriers and arrangements remain visible through descriptive tables and the supplement. Narrowing the number of primary models must not erase substantive domains from the research account.

### Models, references and uncertainty

For owner review, propose unadjusted original-category distributions first; one adjusted engagement specification per primary outcome; at most one substantively nominated extension per outcome. These four planning slots are not approved fits, executable equations or a hidden sensitivity budget. Exact substantive contrasts, covariates, reference levels and extensions remain explicit owner decisions.

The raw context pool is age (`q4`), education (`q2_2`), occupation (`q2_3`), first-arrival calendar history (`q2_1` plus an approved interview-year source) and version-aware residence (`q3`).
