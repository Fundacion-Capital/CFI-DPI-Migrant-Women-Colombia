### What the completed diagnostics establish

The three available two-class comparisons are all **native mismatches**, with no stripe mismatches. H2_k2 is closest in its original label order; H3_k2 and H4_k2 are closest after a scratch swap of the two class columns. Even after those swaps, their full-profile differences are nonzero. Maximum theoretical mixture-pattern differences are about 1.20e-6, 7.68e-8 and 5.44e-7 for H2_k2, H3_k2 and H4_k2, respectively. Thus labels explain much of two raw differences, but neither labels nor export rounding establish exact equality. Both Jacobian coordinate systems have full evaluated rank at relative 1e-8 for these two-class states (31, 29 and 23 free parameters). This is point-specific numerical evidence, not proof of global identification, repeatable optimization or substantive profile validity.

H2_k3 is a **new converged replay state without an archived native target**. Its largest free variance is approximately 4.83e17; covariance rank at relative 1e-8 is 2 while standardized correlation rank is 47. The native-logit Jacobian has rank 43 versus probability-coordinate rank 47, and the evaluated item probabilities include a machine-boundary zero. These are strong coordinate-scaling/boundary warnings, not grounds to promote a newly converged three-class result or to call the historical failed result wrong. An evaluated zero can reflect finite-precision saturation of a finite logit/cutpoint; it is not evidence of a population structural zero.

H3_k4 is a **historical converged target whose single replay did not converge**. Its stored parameter rank is 59, but the original state's covariance rank at relative 1e-8 is 2 versus correlation rank 59, and its native-logit/probability-coordinate Jacobian ranks are 54/59. Its computed minimum covariance eigenvalue is about -261 in a spectrum whose maximum is about 8.77e18. Report both magnitude and scaling: that negative numerical eigenvalue alone does not establish a material negative-information direction in such an extreme spectrum. Machine-boundary item probabilities and the convergence discrepancy remain unresolved scientific holds. No current H3_k4 coefficient/covariance match is claimed, because no converged replay state was saved.

All eight restored states contain a stored gradient, and none contains a stored Hessian. Gradient dimensions, finiteness and maximum magnitude are retained in the complete metric table; missing Hessians are explicitly unavailable, not zero or positive-definite. Neither gradient availability nor a full evaluated probability-coordinate rank resolves boundary regularity, historical-runtime equivalence or global identification. The eight matching archived nonconvergence statuses remain excluded from usable fitted typologies. No fresh posterior metrics, assignments or inferential claims have been borrowed or manufactured.
