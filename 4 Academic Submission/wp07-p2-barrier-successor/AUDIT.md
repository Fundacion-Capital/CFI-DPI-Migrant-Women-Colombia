## Successor scope

The new P2 version is additive and disabled. A source comparison verifies that
its body matches the preserved original apart from the exact 35 deletions:

- 15 classifier commands: three logical families repeated across five versions.
- 10 related count commands.
- 10 unsupported `P2.08` option-4 uncertainty receipt rows.

No other body line, route order, domain, schema, step assignment, sample rule
or field projection was altered. The new first-command `exit 198` hold and its
scope comments are outside that preserved body. CHANGE_CONTRACT.json records
every original line and why it is not inherited.

This is a semantic correction, not a claim of style-only equivalence. “No sé
cómo usarlos” is a substantive barrier in both questions; it can accompany
other named additional barriers. Refusal, duplicate/malformed tokens, later
None combinations and unavailable options retain their original treatment.

The ten false subtype rows are absent from the proposed new receipt set, not
reported as artificial observed zeros. Original P2, old receipt records, source
pins, readers and dispatchers remain unchanged. A future successor binding must
use the changed receipt inventory and new source digest. No reader integration
or operational requalification was attempted here. The full successor retains
its TARGET33-specific checked denominators and is not a general fixture runner.
