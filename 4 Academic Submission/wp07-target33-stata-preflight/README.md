## Transport decision and admission

The sealed V6 inline command channel is limited to 8,192 bytes and sequences 0–2. The adapter/provider also impose a two-call recipe. Offline framing with an artificial scope and key demonstrates that P2 does not fit. Raising the cap, splitting P2, or editing the existing channel is not part of the preflight.

The proposed successor uses a fixed per-stage file reference, not an arbitrary caller path: an authenticated control frame binds stage, byte length, SHA-256, exact run scope, sequence and deadline. Stage names resolve only through a sealed, read-only manifest under the isolated artificial source root. Before admission and at worker read, reject foreign scope, unknown/replayed sequence, path escape/alias, wrong bytes, partial staging and inconsistent size/hash. The worker must execute the same checked bytes, not reopen an unpinned mutable pathname after validation. The fixture wire measurement only shows a short descriptor fits the legacy byte cap; it does not make V6 understand that descriptor or support four sequences.

Require actual producer-to-worker byte/digest agreement and measured transit, parse/execute and output completion. Test changed and truncated bodies, wrong digest/size, foreign stages and replays with artificial files before accepting the design. Never split a source body into additional Stata selections or assume that source length proves parse or transport capacity.

## Time, ownership and independent evidence

- Use one bound monotonic clock domain and an owned identity tuple tied to retained handles, not PID lookup. Start per-step accounting before transport/staging and record dispatch, worker intake, execution, output validation, and final completion. Each selection must complete strictly before its 45-second ceiling and the 180-second work ceiling; one cleanup attempt must close before its 30-second and the 210-second overall ceilings. These remain proposed maxima, not authority to run.
- The cancellation actor must remain usable while the selection transport is intentionally blocked; validate same run, epoch, worker birth identity and job membership before action. Capture request, busy evidence, delivery/stop observation, both exits, zero owned activity, zero retained handles, and closure. Do not accept a timeout or a signal-only receipt. A foreign target or missing independent proof fails closed. No replacement or retry.
- Distinguish successful artificial completion, clean canceled failure, and quarantine for late, incomplete or ambiguous terminal evidence. A clean canceled failure does not promote the four-selection capacity case. No protected wave may inherit either case's scope.

The preflight audit rechecks prior seals, source hashes, exact synthetic projection, artificial wire sizing and valid/invalid clock/cancellation certificates. These are static and mathematical checks, not a real Stata parser, performance test or independent Windows stop test. The required isolated transport/provider and real source-exact acceptance harness remain to be implemented and separately reviewed before seeking launch authority. Confirm the named operators/readers, author-restricted destination access and retention/handover for complete, failed and partial packets before any participant wave.
