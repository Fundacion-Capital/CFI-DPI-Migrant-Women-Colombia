## Scope and method

The Zotero collection **CFI DPI Migrant Women Colombia** was read through the [local Zotero database](https://www.zotero.org/support/dev/client_coding/direct_sqlite_database_access) in query-only mode. The [NotebookLM source list](https://notebook.google.com/notebook/93a7f63a-ef2b-4c7b-ab8d-d0cfae228189) was checked using the supplied public link in an existing browser session: no new login, edits or notebook-generated answers. The check reconciles source identities, not NotebookLM upload bytes. The 31 July legacy draft is the bibliography baseline; the two archive copies of the project's `1 Literature` folder were checked separately. No fieldwork or participant records were opened.

The corpus was screened using title, abstract, selected methods or conclusions when readable, and publisher/issuer sources for important claims. We prioritized research on migrants in Colombia, studies of remittances and gendered control, and primary sources on payment-system design and timing. Targeted searches covered Colombia/PPT/financial access; digitalization among Bogotá migrants; payment adoption in Latin America; Bre-B milestones, open finance and consumer safeguards; and national denominators for connectivity, financial inclusion and remittances. Working papers, designs, country surveys, administrative indicators and press are kept distinct. A source with no direct bearing on the argument need not be cited in the paper.
