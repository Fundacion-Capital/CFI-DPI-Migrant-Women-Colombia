# Colombia/DPI chronology: which conditions preceded fieldwork?

Dates refer to the specified event, not necessarily the first date every provider or consumer could use a service. Primary sources were checked on 4 November 2026. These are contextual statements, not legal advice or a ruling on individual eligibility.

| Date or period | Verified event | What the paper can and cannot infer |
|---|---|---|
| 21 Oct 2014 | [Law 1735 of 2014](https://normograma.dian.gov.co/dian/compilacion/docs/ley_1735_2014.htm) created the framework for specialized electronic deposits and payments companies (SEDPE). | Background to nonbank provision; not the launch of Bre-B or proof of migrant access. |
| 1 Dec 2020 | The [Registraduría circular introducing the digital citizenship ID](https://www.registraduria.gov.co/IMG/pdf/circular-externa-011-2021-presentacion-cc-digital.pdf) records the start of production of the digital-ID documents. On 4 December the [IDB approved CO-T1600](https://www.iadb.org/en/project/CO-T1600), a separate initiative for Venezuelan migrants involving savings/remittances and digital asset exchanges. | The Colombian citizenship credential is not a Venezuelan migrant protection credential. IDB approval proves project design, not outcomes, penetration, or a national cross-border rail. |
| 18 Dec 2020 | [Decree 1692 of 2020](https://www1.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=153787) modified the low-value payments framework. | A regulatory antecedent, distinct from the later immediate-payment interoperability standard and go-live. |
| 1 Mar / 28 Apr 2021 | [Decree 216](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=159606) established the temporary protection statute, including the RUMV and PPT; Resolution 0971 of 28 April implemented it, as confirmed by [Presidential Directive 5 of 2022](https://www1.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=187166). | Registration, holding the permit, identification at onboarding, opening a product and continuing to use it are separate events. PPT is not citizenship and not universal across all international migrants or Colombian returnees. Do not apply 2018 PEP findings to PPT as if the programs were the same. |
| 2022 | [Decree 1297](https://www1.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=190426) established the initial open-finance architecture; the [2026 successor text](https://www.minhacienda.gov.co/documents/d/portal/decreto-no-0368-del-07-de-abril-de-2026) describes the earlier scheme as voluntary. [Decree 1389](https://www.funcionpublica.gov.co/eva/gestornormativo/norma.php?i=191409) separately establishes public-data infrastructure governance. | Consent-based financial information sharing, public-sector data governance, legal ID and payment interoperability are separate functions. Do not present the later mandatory framework as already operational during the interviews. |
| 2023 | Article 104 of Law 2294 underlies [BanRep External Resolution 6 of 31 October 2023 and Circular DSP-465](https://www.banrep.gov.co/es/normatividad/sistemas-pago/pagos-inmediatos-bre-b). The regulator lists historical versions of the circular. | Mandate and standards preceded full operation. Cite the relevant historical instrument for an implementation-period detail; do not project 2026 revisions backwards. |
| 7 Feb 2024 | [DNP publicly presented the National Digital Strategy 2023–2026](https://www.dnp.gov.co/Prensa_/Noticias/Paginas/gobierno-del-cambio-presenta-estrategia-nacional-digital-2023-2026.aspx). The [SFC issued open-finance standards under Circular Externa 004](https://www.superfinanciera.gov.co/publicaciones/10114895/circulares-externas-2024/). | Distinguish the strategy period from its launch date, and standards from working integrations or measured use. |
| 2024 reference year / 2025 publications | [DANE’s ENTIC 2024 bulletin](https://www.dane.gov.co/files/operaciones/ENTIC/bol-ENTICHogares-2024.pdf), chart 6 drawing on ECV 2024, gives household internet connectivity of 65.6% nationally, 72.5% in municipal seats and 41.9% in populated centres/dispersed rural areas. [Global Findex 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/globalfindex/report) uses 2024 interviews; see [methodology](https://thedocs.worldbank.org/en/doc/be6615202d1f08a25855c8ac2d615122-0050012025/related/Survey-methodology.pdf). | Household connectivity, adults' financial behavior and regulator-reported accounts are different universes. None estimates our target group's usage or a post-Bre-B effect. Use the rural number as country context, not a claim about fieldwork geography. |
| 14 Jul 2025 | [BanRep’s education material](https://www.banrep.gov.co/es/bre-b/promocionales-educativos) dates the start of key registration. | Key registration is not a transfer, unique user, active use, or exposure of any survey respondent. |
| 23 Sep–5 Oct 2025 | [BanRep’s rollout notice](https://banrep.gov.co/es/noticias/bre-b-continua-avanzando) dates the controlled pilot. | Pilot and full operation are not interchangeable. Avoid anticipated launch dates in April/July trade reporting. |
| 6 Oct 2025 | [BanRep’s February 2026 technical retrospective](https://www.banrep.gov.co/sites/default/files/publicaciones/archivos/documento-tecnico-bre-b-febrero-2026.pdf), phase 5, records full operation, with 128 entities enabled as participants. Its [consumer FAQ](https://www.banrep.gov.co/es/bre-b/preguntas-frecuentes) describes domestic payments/transfers, with a receiving key distinct from sending requirements. | A domestic payment rail is not a cross-border remittance origin or settlement mechanism. Registered keys, participating institutions, transacting users, transactions and transaction value must never be used interchangeably. A wallet can receive money or facilitate later domestic payments without identifying the upstream remittance rail. |
| 9 Dec 2025–17 Feb 2026 | Recorded interview-date span in the [accepted historical population account](../wp06-author-dossier/D1_POPULATION_ACCEPTED_ACCOUNT_2026-10-19.md). That documentary audit is the source; no data were reopened for WP05. | Fieldwork occurred after launch, about two to four months into operation. This timing is neither assigned exposure nor a comparison across launch. Do not infer dates of first use, household recall periods, pre-launch outcomes or formal administrative closure. |
| Apr 2026 and later | [BanRep’s six-month update](https://www.banrep.gov.co/es/noticias/celebramos-6-meses-bre-b) and [Decree 0368 of 7 April 2026](https://www.minhacienda.gov.co/documents/d/portal/decreto-no-0368-del-07-de-abril-de-2026) are subsequent context. The latter modifies the open-finance system; later implementation deadlines are not resolved in this field-period audit. | Label any figures or policy changes by release and reference date; do not use them to describe participants' earlier choices. |

**Remittance series.** [BanRep’s methodological notes](https://www.banrep.gov.co/es/estadisticas-economicas/series-historicas/tasas-cambio-sector-externo) distinguish incoming transfers to resident households from outgoing transfers, using exchange records and supplementary reports. Its [aggregate series](https://suameca.banrep.gov.co/estadisticas-economicas/tablasPreconstruidas?idDashboard=41403) distinguish reference period, origin, receiving department and modality. If used, pin the exact vintage, definition, unit and coverage. These data do not identify recipients’ gender, legal status, retention of digital balances or linkage to Bre-B.

**Not settled by this audit.** No generic promise of universally free transfers; BanRep’s FAQ assigns fee decisions to the relevant entity. Its [25 July 2025 security guidance](https://www.banrep.gov.co/es/node/64853/printable/print) is evidence of official communication, not fraud incidence; its then-planned launch date is superseded by the retrospective. The SFC's [historical guidance](https://www.superfinanciera.gov.co/publicaciones/10108020/informes-y-cifrasinformesinclusion-financierahistorico-inclusion-financiera-de-los-ciudadanos-venezolanos-presentes-en-el-territorio-colombiano-10108020/) is a route to applicable identification rules, not proof of institution-level practice. Do not promote the [legacy IDEC bill](https://www.camara.gov.co/infraestructura-de-datos-del-estado-colombiano-1017/) to enacted or operational status. Use a dated operator or regulatory source if any draft-tax discussion moves into the final manuscript.
