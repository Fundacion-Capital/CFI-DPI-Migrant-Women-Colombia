\section{Mixed evidence and institutional comparison}
In the joint display, an authored account can show that a survey indicator omits a relevant distinction, such as holding a document versus being allowed to use it. That does not verify a model coefficient or assign a qualitative case to a survey profile. Repeated reports from the same origin are not independent support. We distinguish agreement on a comparable claim, complementary observations, disagreement, and cases that cannot be compared because the source or observation is missing \citep{ocathainMurphyNicholl2010,guettermanFettersCreswell2015}. This integration does not require a pooled likelihood or a causal estimand.

The survey describes reported behavior; the authored accounts describe lived transactions; experts identify implementation problems; primary documents establish selected formal arrangements. Accounts of sending to Venezuela describe an outbound journey, while the survey's remittance outcome concerns what happened after receipt. Reports of faster or more direct inbound routes are other journeys, not matched comparisons. No participant linkage is assumed. The four overlapping qualitative positions and cross-cutting security concerns are not fitted classes.

\paragraph{Sources and units.} The February 2026 Banco de la República technical report documents Bre-B as a domestic immediate-payments scheme during the survey period \citep{banreptechnical2026}. The Banco Central de Reserva del Perú strategy report for the first half of 2025 describes the phased linkage of existing wallets, beginning with Yape and Plin \citep{bcrp2025}. The undated operator descriptions consulted on 19 November 2026 cover distinct units: the Banco Central do Brasil's Pix scheme, settlement platform and alias directory \citep{bcbpix}; the Banco Central de Costa Rica's SINPE Móvil retail service connecting mobile numbers to colón accounts \citep{bccrsinpe}; and Banco de México's SPEI interbank transfer rail \citep{banxicospei} and separate CoDi payment-initiation interface \citep{banxicocodi}. The original DPI Map citation refers to the historical version used in the benchmark, not the current live release \citep{institute_for_innovation_and_public_purpose_ucl_dpi_2025}.

The dated documents and service descriptions establish selected design features and institutional responsibilities. They do not supply comparable evidence on acceptance of migrant documents, usability, remittance delivery, realized costs, effective recourse, gender-disaggregated uptake or the quality of interagency coordination. Where those outcomes are not documented in comparable units, we leave them undetermined rather than assigning a score.
