\section{Determinants results}
\label{sec:det-results-supplement}
Table~\ref{tab:det-models} reports the intended and fitted samples for all five models. Tables~\ref{tab:det-core} and~\ref{tab:det-checks} show the complete set of 15 fixed-endpoint contrasts, with pointwise intervals, observed endpoint counts and support flags. Figure~\ref{fig:determinants-all} shows the same contrasts on a common scale.

The positive IAFF2 contrast for remittance disposition persists in the extension and the common-sample check. The positive IADT2 contrast for domestic participation persists in the extension. Both core comparisons have both exact endpoints in 11 of 12 screened strata. The IAT3 intervals include zero in all five models. For IAFF2 in the domestic models, the point estimates are close even though the D0 interval includes zero and the D1 interval does not; that difference does not test whether the two estimates differ.

Only the remittance-core and remittance-extension IADT2 contrasts have both endpoints in all 12 strata. The other 13 contrasts have at least one stratum in which an endpoint falls outside the observed range. Five of those 13 also have a different stratum where an endpoint is inside the range but is not observed exactly. Neither kind of gap changes the fitted or standardization sample.

Table~\ref{tab:det-coefficients} reports the three focal logit coefficients and their standard errors. Table~\ref{tab:det-wald} gives the six core tests: R0/IAFF2 and D0/IADT2 are the only coefficient nulls rejected at the 5\% level after Holm adjustment. The adjusted test results do not apply to individual probability intervals or tests across specifications.
