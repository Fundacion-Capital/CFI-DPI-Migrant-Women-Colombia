\section{Determinants results}
\label{sec:det-results-supplement}
Table~\ref{tab:det-models} reports intended and fitted samples for the five models. The rank column is the rank of the fitted coefficient covariance matrix, as stored by Stata's logit estimator. Tables~\ref{tab:det-core} and~\ref{tab:det-checks} report all 15 contrasts, pointwise intervals, observed endpoint counts and support flags. Figure~\ref{fig:determinants-all} puts the contrasts on a common scale.

The positive adjusted association between the two-condition score and remittance disposition persists in the extension and common-sample check. The positive association between the ability and confidence score and domestic participation persists in its extension. For each of these two core contrasts, both exact endpoints appear in 11 of 12 education-by-residence strata. An endpoint lies outside the observed range in the remaining stratum, which is still included in each model's average. The access-score intervals include zero in all five models. For the onboarding score in the domestic models, the point estimates remain close even though the D0 interval includes zero and the D1 interval does not. This difference is not a test of whether the estimates differ.

Only the remittance-core and remittance-extension contrasts for IADT2 have both endpoints in all 12 strata. The other 13 contrasts each have at least one stratum in which an endpoint is outside the observed range. For five of these 13, a different stratum has an endpoint inside the range but not exactly observed. Neither circumstance changes the fitted sample or the sample used to average predictions.

Table~\ref{tab:det-coefficients} gives the three focal logit coefficients; Table~\ref{tab:det-wald} gives the six core coefficient-null tests. The Holm-adjusted p-values are below 0.05 for IAFF2 in R0 and IADT2 in D0 only. The adjustment does not apply to the probability intervals or to comparisons across specifications.

\section{Exhibit coverage}
The main paper presents the survey summaries, observed onboarding combinations, core contrasts and exploratory profiles. This supplement presents estimates for the five working logit models, the exploratory measurement comparisons and the documentary source notes. It does not report frequency distributions for every question named in the joint display. Endpoint predictions by subgroup, the full support and covariance records, and coefficient estimates beyond the three focal scores are not included here. The data availability statement describes the access boundary; inclusion of an aggregate does not clear the underlying records for release.
