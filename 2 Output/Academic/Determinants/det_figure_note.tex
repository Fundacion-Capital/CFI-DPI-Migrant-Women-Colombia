Model-standardized higher-minus-lower probability differences in percentage points, not trimmed to supported strata. R0/D0 are the remittance and domestic cores; R1/D1 add occupation and arrival year on the same rows as their cores. R0C uses the remittance core specification on the common sample, which equals the D0 sample. IAT3: reported phone/internet/data access; IADT2: reported transfer ability and fraud-avoidance confidence; IAFF2: own-name address proof and phone registration. The contrast is 0.50 to 0.75 for IAT3 and IADT2, and 0.50 (one condition) to 1.00 (both) for IAFF2. Open markers indicate limited exact support. Strata cross grouped education and recorded residence. The support fraction counts strata containing both exact endpoints. Outside counts strata where either endpoint is beyond the observed range. Full screened coverage does not establish overlap on all covariates. Intervals are pointwise; they use the delta method and the independent-record sandwich covariance. Unrecorded household or referral dependence is not accounted for. Associations are descriptive, not causal.
